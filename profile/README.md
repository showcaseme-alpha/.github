## 🤔 The problem we keep running into

> 📸 A gifted photographer in Vijayawada has a phone full of brilliant work and no place to show it.
>
> 💻 A developer has shipped real projects, but their resume is a PDF nobody opens.
>
> 🛒 A kirana store that has served the same street for twenty years doesn't exist on the internet at all.

Getting online still asks for things most people don't have: 🎨 a designer, 👨‍💻 a developer,
🖥️ hosting, 🔤 English, and ⏳ time. So talent stays invisible, and local businesses lose customers
to whoever showed up first on a search.

## 🛠️ What we make

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎯 Showcaseme
**Portfolios for people who'd rather be doing their work.**

👩‍💻 Developers · 📷 Photographers · 🎨 Designers · and a growing list of other professions.

- 📄 **Start from your resume.** Upload it and your experience, projects, education, languages and
  awards become a portfolio.
- 🎨 **Pick a look, keep your content.** Switch between designed templates without retyping a word.
- 🤖 **Build it by chatting.** Connect Showcaseme to Claude and describe the portfolio you want.
- 📊 **See who's looking.** Visits, the channels they came from and what they clicked.
- 👥 **Teams too.** Organisations give every member a portfolio, with seats managed in one place.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🏪 Showcaseme Business
**A website for every shop in India, in the owner's own language.**

🛒 Kirana stores · 💇 Salons · 🧵 Tailors · 🩺 Clinics · and every small business in between.

- 🎙️ **Just talk.** Answer a few questions by voice or text, in one of 8 Indian languages.
- ✍️ **Written for you.** We draft the description and services; you can add your photos and a
  logo.
- 📞 **Ready for customers.** WhatsApp and call buttons, Google Maps, opening hours and a contact form.
- 🗣️ **Speaks their language too.** Visitors read the site in the language the owner chose.
- 🆓 **Free to start.** Live in minutes, at a link you can share anywhere.
